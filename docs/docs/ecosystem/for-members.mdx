## A Professional Identity That Gets You Paid

Freelancers often need more than a portfolio — they need a way to present their work, share their CV, and get paid, all without stitching together separate tools. Netlink combines a bio link, CV builder, and direct payment channel into one professional presence.

## How Netlink Supports Freelancers

- **CV & Portfolio in One Place** Present your experience, skills, and work samples through a single, shareable Netlink page
- **Direct Client Payments** Accept payments from clients through Netlink Pay, without relying on multiple third-party invoicing or payment tools
- **Always Up to Date** Update your Netlink page as your experience and offerings evolve, keeping your professional identity current

## Built for Simplicity

Netlink removes the technical friction of accepting digital payments. Freelancers can focus on their clients and their craft, while Netlink Pay handles wallet creation and payment processing invisibly in the background.

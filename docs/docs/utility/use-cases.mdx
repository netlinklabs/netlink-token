## Current Stage

At the current stage of development, NET is accessible through self-custody wallets and the legacy Netlink Mini App, while Netlink Pay — the primary intended interface for NET utility — is under active development inside the core Netlink app.

This transitional period allows the Netlink team to observe real usage, validate assumptions, and prepare a smoother, more integrated experience for users going forward.

## Looking Ahead

As Netlink Pay becomes available, NET is expected to power practical, everyday interactions within the Netlink ecosystem — including fee reduction, rewards, and ecosystem access, as outlined in the Utility Overview section.

Over time, this is intended to shift NET's role from a token users manage manually through external wallets, to a utility that works invisibly in the background of everyday Netlink Pay usage.

Any expansion of functionality will be guided by real usage patterns and documented transparently as part of the ongoing development process.

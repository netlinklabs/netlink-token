## How to Verify

- **Total locked balance:** the NET balance of the PinkLock contract can be checked on [PolygonScan](https://polygonscan.com/token/0x0e893B239094A5c573373d44CF1C7D03576b95cb?a=0x6C9A0D8B1c7a95a323d744dE30cf027694710633).
- **Individual locks:** open the PinkLock record links above, or search the transaction hash on PolygonScan.
- **Prices on lock pages:** any price chart shown on third-party lock pages comes from an early DEX test pool with negligible liquidity and does not reflect a market price.

For the full list of multisig wallets and their purposes, see [Multisignature Wallets](/docs/docs/security/multisig-wallets).

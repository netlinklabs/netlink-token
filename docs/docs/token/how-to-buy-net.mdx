At this stage, Netlink Token (NET) is not available for open market trading on any decentralized exchange (DEX) or centralized exchange (CEX).

Previously planned presale phases have been discontinued, and any contributions received were fully refunded to participants. NET is not currently being distributed or sold through any presale, launchpad, or third-party platform.

## Current Status

There is no official channel through which NET can be purchased at this time. Community members should be cautious of any third-party links, presale offers, or platforms claiming to sell NET — these are not affiliated with the Netlink team.

## Future Availability

As the Netlink ecosystem and Netlink Pay mature, an official DEX listing may be introduced, supported by sustainable liquidity funding. Any future listing will be announced exclusively through:

- This documentation page
- The official Netlink website
- Official Netlink communication channels

## Important Notice

Netlink does not provide financial or investment advice. Always verify information through official channels before interacting with any third-party platform claiming association with NET.

Further information regarding NET's role and utility within the ecosystem can be found throughout this documentation.

## Presale History & 2026 Direction

- Netlink conducted three presale rounds in the past: Presale 1 (Pinksale), Presale 2 (Gempad), and Presale 3 (Pinksale)
- All three presales failed to reach their goals and all contributions were fully refunded to participants
- Throughout 2026, the team is not running a public presale program. Development focus is on the core Netlink application
- Token purchase requests are handled case-by-case through the [Investor & Partnership Inquiry form](https://forms.zohopublic.com/labsnetlinkteamgm1/form/InvestorPartnershipInquiry/formperma/sn2mTI_Z151GVLTqLG0mk8Dt5XT4qG_78xr8LckrHNc), structured as a private sale subject to a vesting schedule
- A public presale program is expected to resume after core application milestones are reached, planned for early 2027
- NET is not currently distributed or sold through any public presale mechanism, launchpad, or third-party platform

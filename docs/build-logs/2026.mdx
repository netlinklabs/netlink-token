Following the foundational work completed in 2025, 2026 has focused on legal structuring, ecosystem integrity, and early product development for Netlink Pay.

This page serves as an ongoing public record of development activities and significant changes carried out during this period.

## Legal Foundation

- PT Netlink Labs Global was officially established as the legal entity behind the Netlink ecosystem

---

## Presale Discontinuation

- All planned presale phases, including Phase 2, were discontinued
- All contributions received during Presale Phase 1 were fully refunded to participants
- NET is not currently distributed or sold through any presale mechanism, launchpad, or third-party platform
- This documentation has been updated accordingly to reflect the current distribution status

---

## Netlink Pay Development

- Continued development of Netlink Pay as the built-in Web3 wallet inside the core Netlink app
- Ongoing integration work with fiat on/off-ramp and KYC providers to support mainstream accessibility

---

## Documentation

- Migration of documentation infrastructure to improve clarity, structure, and maintainability
- Whitepaper updated to Version 2.0, reflecting Netlink's evolution toward a unified digital identity and Web3 payments platform

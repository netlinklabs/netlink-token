Privacy Policy

How netlinktoken.com collects and uses information.

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies only to netlinktoken.com, the informational website for the Netlink Ecosystem Token (NET) operated by PT Netlink Labs Global. It is a static, informational site — it does not have user accounts, logins, or wallet connections.

This policy does not cover netlink.bio, the separate Netlink digital identity and Netlink Pay platform, which has its own privacy policy, account system, and KYC/wallet data handling.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to operate this website:

  • Basic visitor analytics — IP address, browser/device information, and cookies collected automatically through our hosting provider as part of normal web traffic logging.
  • Contact and investor form data — if you voluntarily submit the investor/partnership inquiry form, we receive the information you provide (typically name and email address).

We do not collect KYC documentation, wallet addresses, private keys, or any identity-verification data on this site. That data, where applicable, is handled exclusively by netlink.bio under its own privacy policy.

3. Third-Party Services

This website relies on the following third-party services, each of which may process data under its own privacy policy:

Google Fonts

Loads web fonts used on this site.

Vercel

Hosts and deploys this website, and may log standard request/traffic data.

Zoho Forms

Processes submissions from the investor/partnership inquiry form linked from this site.

4. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve this website.
  • To understand aggregate visitor traffic and usage patterns.
  • To respond to investor and partnership inquiries submitted through our contact form.

We do not sell visitor data, and we do not use form submissions for purposes beyond responding to the inquiry submitted.

5. Your Rights

If you have submitted information through our contact or investor form, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of that information at any time by contacting us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@netlinktoken.com.

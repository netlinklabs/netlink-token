About NET

The utility token
behind the Netlink ecosystem.

NET (Netlink Ecosystem Token) is a utility token used inside the Netlink application. It unlocks features, rewards active members, and lets the community take part in the ecosystem's growth.

NET runs on the Polygon network as an ERC-20 token, built for low fees and fast transactions.

Use Cases

Feature Access

Unlock advanced tools inside Netlink, such as premium bio link options and identity features.

Rewards & Loyalty

Earn NET through activity on the platform and redeem it for in-app benefits.

Discounts

Use NET to get reduced pricing on paid Netlink services and upgrades.

Ecosystem Participation

Hold and use NET to take part in the growth of the Netlink ecosystem as new features roll out.

NET Is Not Equity

Netlink operates as two intentionally separate layers: PT Netlink Labs Global (the platform company) and NET (a community utility token on Polygon). As stated in Netlink's Investor Relations disclosure:

"NET is not equity in PT Netlink Labs Global and does not represent ownership, profit-sharing, or any guaranteed value."

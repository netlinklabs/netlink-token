1. Introduction

Netlink is a digital identity platform — bio link, CV builder, and landing page builder in one place. NET is the native utility token of the Netlink ecosystem, built on the Polygon network.

Netlink Pay, the built-in Web3 wallet inside Netlink, connects everyday users to on-chain tools without requiring any crypto experience. NET is designed to power this experience.

2. Ecosystem Overview

Netlink

The core product. Users create a bio link, CV, or landing page to represent themselves online.

Netlink Pay

A sub-feature inside Netlink. A non-custodial Web3 wallet (Sequence WaaS on Polygon), created invisibly behind a familiar login (Google account), with no seed phrase required to get started.

NET Token

The utility token connecting both. NET lives on Polygon and is designed to support activity across the Netlink ecosystem.

3. Vision & Mission

Vision

To make Web3 identity and payments accessible to everyone — not just crypto-native users.

Mission

  • Give professionals (real estate agents, creators, freelancers) a single link that represents their identity and enables direct payments
  • Make wallet creation and crypto payments invisible and frictionless
  • Use NET as the connective utility token across Netlink's products
  • Maintain transparency through the Polygon blockchain

4. Use Cases NET

NET's utility is under active development inside Netlink Pay. Current direction:

  • Fee & Reward Layer — NET is intended to reduce fees or provide rewards for transactions inside Netlink Pay
  • Ecosystem Access — NET holders may receive early access to new Netlink features
  • Community Support — NET can be sent as a signal of support between users
  • Future Integration — as Netlink Pay matures, more concrete utility (staking, discounts, loyalty) will be introduced

This section will be updated as utilities go live.
